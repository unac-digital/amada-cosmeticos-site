# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

static HTML, CSS and JavaScript, explicitly requested for this concept.

## Users

Primary audience inferred from the brief: visitors encountering Amada Cosméticos for the first time and seeking a concise understanding of the brand before making contact. The client representative is Lúcia.

## Product Purpose

Present Amada Cosméticos through a polished first institutional concept. Success means communicating a credible, distinctive brand presence and offering a clear, non-transactional path to begin a conversation.

## Positioning

Open decision: no commercial positioning, product specialization, catalog, proof points, or unique mechanism were provided. The concept must therefore present an editable institutional narrative without implying unsupported facts.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Static institutional website concept in Portuguese.
- Contact call to action must not collect or submit visitor data.
- Do not invent a phone number, address, catalog, social profiles, prices, product or sustainability claims, testimonials, reviews, or legal data.
- Semantic HTML, responsive behavior, keyboard access, strong focus states, skip link, accessible navigation, and correct image alternatives are required.
- Content is intentionally generic and editable until Lúcia supplies approved brand facts.

## Brand Commitments

- Confirmed name: Amada Cosméticos.
- Confirmed representative: Lúcia.
- Voice inferred for this concept: warm, clear, composed, and free of promotional overclaiming.
- No logo, palette, typography, photography, or other visual assets were provided.

## Evidence on Hand

Only the business name, representative name, project intent, and constraints in the brief. No commercial claims, catalog, photographs, testimonials, contact coordinates, legal copy, or brand guidelines are available; future work must not fabricate them.

## Product Principles

1. Make the brand understandable without pretending unknown facts are settled.
2. Use editorial confidence rather than unsupported claims.
3. Keep every visible text block easy for the client to replace.
4. Make the route to contact clear without collecting data.
5. Treat accessibility and responsive behavior as part of the concept, not cleanup.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The site must support keyboard navigation, visible focus, readable hierarchy, a skip link, an accessible responsive menu, semantic structure, and reduced-motion preferences.
